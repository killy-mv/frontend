Cookies

Small strings the browser stores per site and attaches to every request. document.cookie is one of the strangest APIs in the platform: reading gives you everything, writing sets one.

Cookies are tied to an origin. Opened from file:// this page has no origin, so nothing will be stored. Serve the folder over http (npx serve, or python -m http.server) to see the examples work.
