Date Get Methods

Reading the parts of a date. Every one of these reports in local time unless you use the UTC variant.

The full set

getDate() is the day of the month. getDay() is the weekday. getYear() is a deprecated relic that returns the year minus 1900 — never use it.

Turning numbers into names

There is no getMonthName(). Use an array, or better, toLocaleDateString.
