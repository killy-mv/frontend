ES2016 – ES2018

After the enormous ES6, the committee switched to a yearly train: whatever is ready ships in June. These three years are small, and every one of them is something you use daily.

ES2017 — async/await

ES2017 — the rest

ES2018 — object spread and rest

ES2018 — asynchronous iteration

ES2018 — Promise.finally
