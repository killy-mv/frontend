Number Properties

Constants that describe the limits of the number type. They live on Number itself, not on individual numbers.

The constants

MAX_VALUE and overflow

Go past the maximum and you get Infinity — no error, no wrap-around.

MAX_SAFE_INTEGER

"Safe" means every integer up to it can be represented exactly, and no two integers share a representation.

EPSILON

The gap between 1 and the next representable number. Use it as a tolerance when comparing decimals.

They belong to Number, not to numbers
