Bitwise

Operators that work on the individual bits of a number. JavaScript converts its 64-bit floats to 32-bit signed integers first, then converts back.

The operators

Everything becomes a 32-bit integer

Flags in a single number

The classic use: pack several booleans into one integer, one bit each.

Colours

A 24-bit colour is three 8-bit channels packed together. Shifts and masks pull them apart.

Tricks you will see in the wild

Genuine uses

  • Permission and feature flags stored as one integer.
  • Colour channel packing and unpacking.
  • Hashing, checksums and binary file formats.
  • Working with TypedArray data from a canvas or a socket.
For integers larger than 32 bits, use BigInt — its bitwise operators have no width limit.
