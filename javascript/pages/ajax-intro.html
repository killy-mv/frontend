Requests do not come back in the order you sent them. This is the single most common AJAX bug.

What AJAX cost us

Fetching in the background breaks things the browser used to handle for free: the URL no longer describes what is on screen, Back does not undo the last action, and a screen reader is not told that anything changed. Update the URL with the History API, and announce changes with aria-live.
